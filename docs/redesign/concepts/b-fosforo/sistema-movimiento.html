Motion: specification

Fast and quiet, like a good instrument: nothing over 360 ms, ease-out for everything that arrives, and no animation at all for what a keyboard repeats a hundred times a day.

Ceiling 360 ms for anything the user waits on. Exits are 60 % of the entrance. Loops (LED, skeleton) are decorative and always stoppable.

prefers-reduced-motion

Fewer and gentler, not none. Movement and scale go; opacity and color stay, because they explain state. Every travel distance is a CSS variable (--move-y, --scale-hover) that becomes 0 / 1, so keyframes keep working as pure fades. Loops stop: the LED keeps its ring, the timer keeps counting, the skeleton stays dim. Nothing important is conveyed by motion alone.
