Your library starts here

Afterplay keeps your games, sessions and notes in one place, and times your play on its own. Three steps fill it.

1

Add your API keys

To search games and fetch artwork, Afterplay needs your own IGDB and SteamGridDB keys. Everything else already works.

2

Point at your game folders

Afterplay scans them to find each game's folder and executable, so you do not browse for them by hand.

3

Add your first game

Search by name, or pick one the scan already found. Time played starts counting the next time you launch it.
