Fixed frame: top bar 56, Now playing 72, window gutter 28, control heights 28 / 34 / 44, list row 48. In Tailwind the base is --spacing: 4px, so p-5 = 20.

Radii

xs · 2
meter segments, bars
sm · 4
controls, covers, kbd
md · 8
panels, menus, dialogs, toasts, sheet
full
LED, chips, switch

Nested radii: a 4 px control inside an 8 px panel keeps 12 px of padding, so the curves never fight.

Icons

Lucide, 18 px, stroke 1.6, round caps; 16 px in menus and inputs; filled only for Play and Stop.

Units shrink, digits don’t. In a figure the unit is 0.45em, weight 600, Dust, with 2 px / 6 px of air. Zero-pad minutes (10m, 05m).
Tracking. Big Shoulders below 24 px gets +0.02em, above that +0.005em. Barlow never gets tracking. No uppercase labels, no eyebrows.
TV. Same families at ×1.5: body 21, small 20 (floor), title 42, page 66. Figures never below 28.
